For developersSources, API, CLI & verification limits

The authoritative starter list is subscriptions.json (schema). Registry and orientation data are discovery inputs, not automatic admission or authenticated trust. Other publishers can propose a public feed through a normal reviewed contribution, or fork their own profile.

Browser checks cover the latest record and its immediate predecessor, or one genesis record. They check complete fetched input, native hashes, stream identity, sequence, HEAD binding and the observed adjacent link. They do not fetch the whole chain.

The checker supports native DOGG's bounded, duplicate-free, safe-integer JSON profile and unsigned non-swarm records. It is not frozen RAPP/1 conformance, signature verification, registry/genesis authority, or proof of truth. Historical spec: "rapp/1" declarations do not change those limits. Each checked container exposes its raw-byte SHA-256.

Only followed feeds are periodically checked, at most once per five minutes while the page is visible. Failures keep the last-good observation with an error, never a fresh badge. Selections stay in localStorage when allowed; otherwise they last for this tab only. Public hosts still see ordinary read requests, but no following profile is uploaded.
